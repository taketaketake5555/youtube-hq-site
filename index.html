youtube-hq

A private, read-only reporting tool for one person's own YouTube channels.

What it does

youtube-hq reads statistics from the YouTube channels owned by the person who operates this tool, and turns them into a short daily summary for that same person. It reads view counts, subscriber counts, watch time and audience retention.

It is read-only. It cannot upload, edit or delete videos, and it cannot change any channel setting.

Who can use it

Only the operator. youtube-hq is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups. Nobody else's data is collected.

How it uses Google data

See the Privacy Policy for the data it reads, where it is kept and how to revoke access.
